How It Works

From Field to Verified Record

Every step in the agricultural supply chain is captured, graded, and approved — creating an immutable digital trail.

Collection

Digital weighbridge passes are issued at every mandi collection point. Gross weight, tare weight, and moisture content are captured and cryptographically locked in real time.

Grading

Produce is graded A, B, or C by certified field operators. Photo evidence is attached, and statistical anomaly detection flags outliers automatically — preventing grade manipulation.

Farmer Approval

Farmers receive transparent payout summaries tied to verified weights and grades. Disputes are resolved through evidence-based adjudication with append-only correction records.
